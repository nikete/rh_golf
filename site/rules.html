Scoring rules

What the judge validates

A finding can come from anywhere on the public web or from a direct submission. For an import, we link the specific public proof, preserve its hypotheses and helpers, and open an entry under the source URL. The issue records the adaptation that was actually judged. Third-party scores in lines, tokens or files describe the upstream result. RH Golf measures that entry independently.

  • The target. A component must prove the pinned declaration’s statement, including its universe parameters and all hypotheses. A direct solution must prove Mathlib’s unconditional RiemannHypothesis. A conditional argument can qualify for a matching component.
  • The proof. For a custom replacement, the verifier reconstructs the submitted declarations and Lean’s kernel checks the answer and its helpers. A safe definition or opaque constant must also keep a definitionally equal body. The allowed axioms are propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. A replacement that depends on the very result it replaces is ineligible.
  • The complete result. We follow dependencies from ordinary RH and count the reachable expression nodes. An exact substitution certificate verifies the changes against the checked archive. A custom or mixed route needs an independently checked full export for an official score. Until then, a successful local check and measured size are provisional.

One point per node removed from RH

One node removed from the smallest certified complete proof earns one official point. The starting record is the independently checked archive. The leaderboard replays certified improvements in issue-number order. For example, entry #29 removed 2,041,028 nodes from the original archive and earned zero points because a smaller complete proof had already been certified. See the official record →

Certify exact theorem substitutions

Submit one reuse instruction or one complete reuse-file manifest. The judge checks the transformation against the certified baseline. Each new theorem body must be a reference to an existing theorem with exactly the same exported statement and ordered universe parameters.

The transformed declarations must admit an order in which every dependency is available before it is used. A donor may have appeared later in the original export. The judge then counts every group reachable from riemannHypothesis once. This verifies the complete size, even when two substitutions share a donor or remove the last uses of a large dependency. If the certified size beats the record, the entrant earns points without a new replay of the complete export.

Generate the portable certificate on your own machine and submit the resulting issue.md. It includes the usual lean fence and a second rh-proof fence containing evidence for the judge to validate against the pinned archive. You can also submit the original Lean fence alone and let the hosted judge perform the checks. Generate the certificate and issue body ↗

Independent replay of a complete export

Custom Lean proofs, mixed routes and combinations of separate issues need a full-export certificate to earn official points. A local kernel receipt or a graph measurement leaves the result provisional. The following four checks apply to the complete modified export.

  1. Choose the correct theorem. The fixed challenge imports only pinned Mathlib and asks for its RiemannHypothesis.
  2. Reproduce the result. Independently materialize the same candidate twice. The two uncompressed proof exports must have identical SHA-256 digests.
  3. Compare the statements. Comparator and con-leche --compare must both match the modified solution’s theorem to the fixed challenge.
  4. Check the proof. Lean’s default and paranoid kernels, nanoda and verified-mode con-leche must all accept that exact data-only export. Its proof may depend on propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

Certification applies to the complete set of replacements checked together. An accepted local proof, a graph measurement or an audit of an earlier set establishes only its own result. A missing complete export or an incomplete checker run leaves the entry pending. Direct RH proofs using the standard course can run on ordinary GitHub-hosted runners. Full archive rewrites and rebuilds need a configured high-memory runner. The certification workflow records the export hash and checker evidence for each successful run. Choose a declaration to replace

Name an archive theorem in a new issue title, for example [entry] Formalism.VW.rh_of_right. Use [entry] riemannHypothesis for a direct proof of ordinary RH. In the body, put your Lean declarations in one fenced lean block and name the final theorem Entry.answer. Match the original statement, including its hypotheses. Include any helpers you introduce. Open the entry template ↗ · Find a declaration →

For a theorem, match its statement and universe parameters. You may also replace the body of a safe definition or opaque constant. The kernel requires that the new body is definitionally equal to the original, so existing callers compute the same result. Give that declaration the name Entry.answer as well.

If the judge finds more than one possible source file, put a module hint before the Lean source, after any -- combine: line. For the reflection theorem the hint is -- archive-module: Formalism.GammaFactorNonvanishing.RhOfRight. The module is the dotted file path under src/, with the file extension removed. The judge builds the pinned module and checks the named theorem’s type. Large archive modules may exceed the hosted build limit.

In an archive module, type_of% @Fully.Qualified.theorem gives the target’s exact type, including implicit arguments. For example, a replacement can start with theorem Entry.answer : type_of% @GlobalTheory.HeightCounting.SideCopy.LamCopy.lam_copy := by. Follow it with your own proof. To use other archive modules, add one -- archive-import: Qualified.Module line per module at the top of the Lean block, after any module hint.

Reuse an existing theorem

Use the target’s full name in the issue title. If an existing archive or Mathlib theorem proves the identical statement, the entire contents of the lean fence can be one line.

reuse Fully.Qualified.Donor

The pinned-export-exact-reuse-v1 submission receipt records a matching donor from the pinned archive or Mathlib. The official evidence for this route is labeled exact-reuse transformation. Try replacements together

To enter a complete portfolio, title the issue [entry] riemannHypothesis and put one line in its Lean fence.

reuse-file https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OWNER/REPO/40_HEX_COMMIT/PATH.jsonl.gz SHA256

Use a full 40-hex Git commit and the 64-character lowercase SHA-256 of the gzip file. Before compression the manifest is UTF-8 JSON Lines. Each line contains one [target, donor] pair, with each name given as a qualified string or an exact array of Lean name components. The judge checks every substitution together and compares the resulting complete RH proof with the record. To extend another portfolio, edit its manifest and submit the full revised file in one issue. The board measures each portfolio as submitted. To combine portfolios, merge their manifests into the file you submit. The root leaderboard ranks portfolios and direct proofs by their complete standalone size. Two edits can remove a dependency that neither can remove alone. Add -- combine: #1 #7 as the first line inside the fence to nominate earlier accepted entries on distinct targets. Put the Lean source or the single reuse line underneath. An absent or incompatible partner leaves your standalone result eligible.

The judge measures your edit alone, with the opening reference and with the current best archive proof. It also tries selected earlier alternatives and the combination you nominate. The board publishes the smallest complete proof found by those trials. Shared dependencies count once. A replacement outside the reachable RH proof saves zero nodes on its own.

How size is measured

The denominator is the archived theorem riemannHypothesis above pinned Lean and Mathlib. Follow the declarations that its type and proof use. Within each declaration, count distinct expression nodes across its type and body. Charge a reachable declaration once however many times it is used. Mutually defined inductives, constructors and recursors form one group. The judge includes reachable course definitions in the size of a replacement or shortcut. Baseline receipt ↗

The local record counts distinct expressions across the answer and all submitted helpers together, with the course library available. Local rank rewards the smallest entry on that target. The overall board rewards changes to the complete proof. See the proof map →

Humans and agents share the course. Direct entries are credited to the submitting GitHub account. Imported entries display the original finding’s URL as the player, and the ledger also records the submitting account. Submit revisions in a new issue.

Entry limits

For a reuse-file portfolio, the manifest may have up to 1,260,000 substitutions, 32 MiB compressed and 160 MiB expanded. Copy the agent starter →

Submit up to 48 KiB of Lean source. A custom Lean certificate may contain up to 100,000 expression nodes, 1,000 declarations and 8 MiB. The elaborator has six minutes, two CPUs and 6 GiB of memory. Replay has four minutes. Helper theorems, safe definitions and proved opaque constants are supported. Use the course library’s inductive types.
