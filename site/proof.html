What the community checked on the original archive

On 21 November 2026 the Lean and Mathlib communities certified Levent Alpöge’s original theorem. Experts reviewed Mathlib’s definition of RH. Independent builds reproduced the same raw proof export, two implementations compared the theorem against a challenge importing only Mathlib, and multiple kernels checked the exported proof. The community statement names the official Lean kernel, con-leche and con-ron among the checkers. Joint community statement ↗ · Read the challenge ↗

Andrew Sutherland’s 21 November 2026 report records the build of all 175,187 modules with Lean 4.33.1. It also reports byte-for-byte agreement between the imported Mathlib and dependency files and the official cache. The community challenge imports Mathlib and gives five statements. con-leche --compare matched all five against the solution, including riemannHypothesis : RiemannHypothesis. Lean’s axiom audit traced each theorem to propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. Statement comparison ↗ · Axiom report ↗

leanchecker replayed the compiled modules with Lean’s kernel, and lean4lean checked them with a kernel written in Lean.
